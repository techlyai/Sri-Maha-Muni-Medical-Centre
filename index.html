About Us · எங்களைப் பற்றி

Committed to Your Health and Well-being

Sri Maha Muni Medical Centre is a computerized blood test centre on Alanganallur Road, Umachikulam, Madurai. We bring laboratory testing and ECG together with home nursing, physiotherapy and foot reflexology, so families in and around Umachikulam can find dependable everyday care close to home.

Every visit is handled with patience and respect — from a clear explanation before your sample is taken to a computerized report you can easily read and share with your doctor.
